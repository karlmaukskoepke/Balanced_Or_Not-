Printable sheets

Students use these alongside the page. Equation numbers match the page exactly.

  • Solving sheet (PDF, 2 pages) All 12 equations, with the slider line and space to solve each one.
  • Reflection page (PDF, 1 page) The three columns (One solution, Infinite solutions, No solutions) and prompts 1 to 5c.
  • Practice sheet (PDF, 1 page) Six more equations to solve and sort, with a group talk question about “infinitely many.”

Walking the class through it

  • Open the activity at equation 1 For the projector. Every numbered equation along the top can be clicked.
  • Reset this computer and open equation 1 Clears all saved progress on this computer (the same as "Start over").
  • Balance test page Pick any of the 12 equations, pre-built, and slide x freely.

The page is built for one Chromebook per group. Progress is saved on each computer; when the page opens it asks "Where is your group starting?" (it suggests the last equation opened there). "Start over" (top right of the activity) clears it. Keys: N for next equation, B for back.
